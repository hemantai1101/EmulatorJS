## Rules from guides to check in training

- **Robot troops can cross mountains.** A player reports this explicitly. The prior claim that they cannot was wrong. Check the move cost with a fresh turn and enough action points; do not infer a terrain ban from one blocked path. [Player account](https://game-bakari.hatenablog.com/entry/2020/12/30/072345)
- **Capture uses zero action points.** A unit can move or fight before capturing. High level infantry can take a full town in one capture action. Keep the unit active until its capture order is done. [Play guide](https://ww3.tiki.ne.jp/~weathercock/game/global3.htm)
- **Only infantry captures bases.** Heavy bombs, cruise missiles, large naval guns, and large rockets can destroy bases instead. A wiki lists a full base size of 16 and a full level 20 infantry squad as enough for one capture. Check how losses change capture strength. [Game wiki](https://wikiwiki.jp/globalforce/技/小技)
- **Automatic fire needs range and sight.** In stages with limited sight, other enemy units or sites can spot our unit for the shooter. It also spends ammo during the opponent's turn. Check ammo at the start of every turn. [Sight guide](https://wazap.com/cheat/迎撃されない/534555/), [Game wiki](https://wikiwiki.jp/globalforce/技/小技)
- **Square ends a unit from its order menu.** Do not press it while an invalid path or warning is still selected. [Control tip](https://wazap.com/game/4542/攻略/)

Exact mountain costs, transport steps, resupply rules, and the best route on the oil field map still need tutorial checks. No guide for that exact stage was found.
