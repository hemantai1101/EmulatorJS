# Harvest Moon notes

Research notes for the Harvest Moon: Back to Nature plugin (`hosting/ps1/plugins/hm-stamina-bar.js`). RAM addresses, what the game's code does, and what has or has not been checked.

## Mobile panel

on touch screens or pages up to 900px wide, only stamina, fatigue and **More** show at first. **More** is a small text link that opens the full panel; **Close** shrinks it. Basket starts with just its count and bar; tap its row to show or hide items and worth. The full view scrolls and keeps Close in reach. Tap **Hearts** to open the girls' hearts. Desktop keeps the full view and tap-box shortcut. On 10 November 2026, `make check` and separate muted desktop Chrome tests passed at 852×393, 568×320 and 390×844 with touch emulation, plus 1280×800 with a mouse. Small view cleared L1/L2/D-pad and L2 received touch input; More/Close and Hearts worked. Preview screenshots used the supplied game photo as a still and fake RAM. No real iPhone check. Deployed as `ps1-player-00123-474` on 10 November 2026; live index and plugin files matched the build with both isolation headers kept.

## Stamina and fatigue

Plugin `plugins/hm-stamina-bar.js` (Harvest Moon stamina bar) is written here. It shows stamina and fatigue bars for Harvest Moon: Back to Nature (US, SLUS-01115) and only reads RAM. Addresses come from public cheat lists: stamina `0x80071A12`, max stamina `0x80071A14`, fatigue `0x80071A16` (u16). On 9 November 2026 a console read of the user's live game gave stamina 100, max 100, fatigue 18. The game's code caps fatigue at 100 (clamp at `0x800D62EC`; at 6 AM a value of 100 or more becomes 99) and changes behaviour at 50, 70 and 80 (`0x800CD4E4`). No code raises that cap, so the Mystic Berry does not change the bar's top. Fatigue shows amber from 50 and red from 80. Other cheat-list addresses (not built into a plugin): money u32 `0x80071A5C`, month `0x80070CF2`, day `0x80070CF4`, hour `0x80070CF8`. `make check` runs `hm-stamina-bar.test.cjs` with fake RAM. The box was checked with fake RAM in the built-in browser. On 9 November 2026 it was also pasted into a separate muted Chrome tab of the live site that started the user's last game (Harvest Moon, Spring 3, 6 AM): it showed Stamina 100/100 and Fatigue 18. The game was not played, so a stamina drop was not seen yet. The tab was closed within about a minute. The day value is 0-based (RAM 2 = day 3).

## Girls' hearts

Tapping the box (or Enter) opens the girls' hearts; localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-hearts-open` keeps that choice. Affection is u16 at `0x80076224 + villager * 0x1D4`; the girls' villager numbers (Karen 3, Ann 7, Elli 9, Mary 14, Popuri 20) come from the table at `0x80055988`. Heart steps come from the game's code at `0x80022F1C`: below 5000, 20000, 30000, 40000, 50000, 60000, then 60000 and up (7 levels). The colour names (black, purple, blue, green, yellow, orange, red) come from fan guides, not the code. Mary's 0 was read from the address the game's own code uses. On 9 November 2026 a chat with Mary in a muted test tab (saving blocked) set `0x80071A22` (the villager being talked to) to 14, confirming Mary is villager 14. That chat changed no affection value, so a plain chat may not add points, at least not a second one on the same day. The game's add-affection function at `0x8011FF0C` (overlay code seen in the library scene) adds to the same `+0x19EC` field for any villager and caps it at 65535. All seven saved Harvest Moon states up to 9 November 2026 21:20 had Mary (villager 14) at 0; her `+0x1A` flag word has bit `0x400` set, which an event sets together with affection at `0x800EA150`. The user reported a Mary event worth +2000 points; it was not found in those saves.

## Held tool and water

Held count (found 11 November 2026 from the game's code, read only): the item menu draw at `0x800FB510` shows a slot's u16 at +4 as a count when `0x800D583C` returns 2. That is byte +2 of the item's 40-byte record behind the pointer at `0x8006C858` (the same table the Basket uses). In an old RAM dump of the user's game, type 2 was items 34-54 (the seeds), 80, 296-298, 376 and 377. Stacking at `0x800FB418` caps the count at 99. The Holding line adds ` ×n` for type-2 items with a count from 1 to 99. Not yet seen with seeds in the user's game. Chicken records hold `0x10A` for a chick at +0 (0x16 bytes before love); the Animals list shows chicks as 🐣 and hens as 🐔.

Water: items are 6-byte slots (u16 id, u16 value at +4). The held item is at `0x80071A84`; the 24 tool slots start at `0x80070EC2`. Tool item numbers 1-57 (1-5 sickles, 6-10 hoes, 11-15 axes, 16-20 hammers, 21-25 cans, then animal tools, seeds, Ocarina, Fishing Rod, Fishing Pole) come from the Harvest Moon Paradise GameShark page (https://harvestmoonparadise.com/hmpb/page.php?id=32); the basic tools match the user's saves. That page also lists `80071A88 0064` as Watering Can Always Full. The plugin's Holding line shows this name. The basic watering can is item 21 and its value is the water left: 0 at the start, 100 after filling, minus 1 per watering (from the user's saves on 9 November 2026). On 10 November 2026 the user's upgraded can was held as item 25 (Mystrile Can) with value 100 when full and value field +2 = 4. The Water line now treats items 21-25 the same, as water left out of 100; how fast upgraded cans drop per use has not been seen. Deployed as `ps1-player-00093-xqn`; see DEPLOYMENT.md.

## Mine floors

Mine (read from the user's save on 9 November 2026, in a muted test tab with saving blocked; not in the plugin): each floor is a 10 x 10 grid. Header at `0x8012D110` (u16): +0 current floor, 0-based (went 0 to 1 when the user took the stairs; a reset clears all 10 floors' grids), +2 stairs chance in percent (10 on a new floor, +2 after each miss), +4/+6 a row/col used to keep stairs away on kinds other than 0, +8/+A the found stairs' row/col, +C one byte per kind set once stairs are found. Tile state bytes start at `0x8012D126 + floor * 100`, index row * 10 + col: 0 not dug, 1 dug hole, 2 stairs. The hoe handler at `0x8011337C` (overlay code) decides what a tile holds only when it is dug: edge tiles give a plain hole, other tiles roll `rand(100) < chance` for stairs while none are found. So nothing hidden is stored before digging. This block stays in memory outside the mine. The place number is a u16 behind the pointer at `0x800A0378` (pointer was `0x8009F78C` in every save). The hoe digs mine tiles only at places 55 and 67 (check at `0x800C64DC`); the user's mine saves read 67 and two farm-time saves read 19. The plugin's Mine line shows `Floor <+0 plus 1>` only at 55 or 67. Do not use `0x8006C3FA`: it changed between 25 and 100 while the player stood still. Deployed as `ps1-player-00098-rs6`.

## Weather

Weather is u16: today at `0x8007AD00`, tomorrow at `0x8007AD02`. In the user's ten saves of 9 November 2026 (Spring 6 to 10), each day's tomorrow value became the next day's today value, and tomorrow's value stayed the same from 6 AM to night. Values 0 Sunny and 1 Rain were seen; 2 Snow, 3 Typhoon and 4 Blizzard come from a public cheat list (vcgamers.com) and are not checked. The plugin's Tmrw line shows an emoji (☀️ 🌧️ ❄️ 🌀 🌨️) with the name as a tooltip, and hides for other values. How the game picks tomorrow's weather has not been traced.

## Mine items

Mine items (read from the user's save of 9 November 2026 at Spring 14; the code and tables were in memory on the farm): when a dug tile is not stairs, `0x80113A20` (overlay code) rolls `rand(100)` (`0x80012A34`) and walks 8 slots, adding each slot's percent until the roll is below the sum. Percents are s16, 8 per floor, at `0x80127FE8 + floor * 16` for place 67 and `0x80128088 + floor * 16` for place 55. Item numbers are at `0x80128128` (67: 0, 102, 312, 124, 123, 122, 96, 103) and `0x80128138` (55: 0, 312, 102, 96, 97, 98, 103, 99). Item 0 is nothing. At 67, item 103 is given once (flag 97); at 55, 103 once (flag 98) and 99 once (flag 96); a repeat gives nothing. Place 67 floor 1 is 40/30/20/10 for slots 0-3; floor 10 is 0/0/10/30/25/20/10/5. Place 55 floor 9 is 0/0/0/0/25/25/0/50, and its floor 10 row starts with 100 (always nothing). Item names come from Almar's Guides CodeBreaker item list (hex ids, read from a 21 March 2024 web.archive.org copy; the live page returns 404): 96 Mystrile, 97 Orichalcum, 98 Adamantite, 102 Coin, 103 Power Berry, 122 Gold, 123 Silver, 124 Copper, 312 Junk Ore. Item 99 is not on that list; the plugin calls it Rare item. The game's own name list in memory on the farm (u16 text, letter = value + 65, `0xFFFF` ends a name) held food names but no ores. The plugin shows each floor's odds under the Mine line from copies of these tables; starred items are given once. Edge tiles and tiles within 2 of found stairs give a plain hole with no item roll.

## Shops

Shops: the weekday is u16 at `0x80070CF6` (0 Sunday; it went up by one each day in the user's saves, and the day-name code at `0x8001AAB8` lists "Sun" first; its caller at `0x800FB73C` reads this address), season at `0x80070CF2`, hour at `0x80070CF8`. The plugin's "Shops" toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-shops-open`) lists shop hours with a green dot when open now. Hours come from fan guides (harvestmoonbacktonatureguide.com shops page; Supermarket 9 AM to 6 PM from the fan wiki), not the game's code, and guides disagree on the Supermarket and Saibara. Festivals, storms and Saibara's earlier hours with friendship are not handled.

## Animals

Animals: love is a byte; addresses from Almar's Guides CodeBreaker animal codes (archived copy): chickens from `0x80074302` every `0xB4` (10 slots, up to the barn), barn animals from `0x80074A0A` every `0xB8` (20 slots), dog `0x800759FA`, horse `0x80075AEA`. The name is 9 u16 letters 18 bytes before the love byte, padded with `0xFFFF`; empty slots start with `0xFFFF`. In the user's saves up to Spring 15 only the dog ("Bin") was named; its love went 19, 23, 17, 11, 8, 10 over Spring 10 to 15. The plugin's Animals toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-animals-open`) lists named slots whose last name cell is `0xFFFF`, as a bar with `love/255`. Chicken and barn slots have not been seen with animals in them, and cows and sheep are not told apart.

## Villagers

Villagers (found 10 November 2026 from the user's ten saves, read only): other villagers' friendship is the u16 at `0x80076222 + villager * 0x1D4`, 2 bytes before the girls' affection field. The game's add function at `0x8011FEE0` (just before the affection adder at `0x8011FF0C`; same base `0x80074838`, field `+0x19EA`) caps it at 255. The girls' own `+0x19EA` field was 0 in every save, and the other villagers' `+0x19EC` field was 0. Saved values ran about 46 to 109 and rose over the saves for some villagers (villager 8 from 70 to 109, Barley and May together from 47 to 76 and 77). Names come from the vcgamers.com GameShark list (its "Citizen Affection" codes write this byte); every code address fits `0x80076222 + n * 0x1D4` exactly, and its girl codes (Karen `800767A0` and so on) match the game's girl table. Names were not checked one by one in the game (no name list is in RAM; the talk target `0x80071A22` was `0xFFFF` in every save). The plugin's Villagers toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-villagers-open`) lists 27 of them with a bar and `value/255`: 1 Jeff, 2 Sasha, 4 Saibara, 5 Gray, 6 Doug, 8 Doctor, 10 Carter, 11 Cliff, 12 Basil, 13 Anna, 15 Barley, 16 May, 17 Thomas, 18 Lillia, 19 Rick, 21 Duke, 22 Manna, 23 Ellen, 24 Gotz, 25 Kai, 28 Harris, 29 Kano (the list says "Canoe"), 30 Gourmet (list: "Gourmand"), 31 Louis, 32 Greg, 34 Stu, 35 Won. Villager 8 is the Doctor: on 10 November 2026 the user gave him a gift, and the auto-save during that talk had the talk target `0x80071A22` = 8 with friendship 160; the next save (4 seconds later) had 169. No other villager's friendship changed then. The cheat list's Doctor address `0x800990C2` does not fit the layout and is not used. The Doctor was added to the list in `ps1-player-00120-6dq`. Not listed: villagers 0, 26, 27 and 33 have no name on the list; 36 ("Your Baby") is left out; the Harvest Sprites' addresses do not fit the layout. Deployed as `ps1-player-00114-gc4`.

## Harvest Sprites

Harvest Sprites (found 10 November 2026 from the user's saves, read only): 7 records of `0xF8` bytes from `0x8007A638` (code at `0x8010C6A0` clears 1736 bytes there; loops at `0x8010C95C` walk 7 records). Each record holds the sprite's name at +4 (u16 letters, `0xFFFF` ends) and friendship at +0x16; the daily code at `0x8010CB6C` adds or takes points and `0x8010CC78` keeps it within 0-255. Names read from the records: Chef, Nappy, Timid, Hoggy, Staid, Bold, Aqua (in that order); these match the vcgamers.com codes ("Diapers" there is Nappy). All seven read 50 in the saves up to 10 November 02:59 and 70 in the 03:48 save. The plugin reads each name from RAM and lists the sprites under their own Sprites toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-sprites-open`), apart from the Villagers list; each bar uses the sprite's outfit colour from fan guides (Chef red, Nappy orange, Hoggy yellow, Timid green, Aqua blue, Staid indigo, Bold purple). Deployed as `ps1-player-00116-k4r`.

## Horse race

Horse race (found 10 November 2026 from the disc's code and the user's Spring 18 save, read only): 3 races of 6 horses, 0xE bytes each from `0x8007AD4E` (race * 0x54): u16 id, speed, whip, style, push, odds. The current race is u16 at `0x8007AD4C` (0-2; 3 means the player rides, using row 2 with id `0xFFFF`). Code at `0x8002206C` rolls them at a new game and on race day (the user's Spring 17 and Spring 18 saves held different horses): speed from per-lane ranges at `0x80055944`, whip 0-255 at random, style and push fixed per horse id (`0x800558A4`), odds = 1 + (best speed - speed)^2 / 1000 + rand(4). The race is mini-game 7 (archive part 17 of `A_FILE.BIN`, loaded at `0x800B7E20`, which then holds 7 and `0x800B7EB8`); betting runs in town code, so that sign is not there at the bet screen. Every frame each horse starts at base pace 128 + 512 * speed / 25500; every 6th frame stamina +5, each horse decides to whip (table at `0x800BB710`, style picks early or late pushing, push applies when another horse is within `0x5A000` ahead), and a whip costs 40 stamina and adds pace * (whip + 150) / 255 for that frame. Position adds pace * 4096 / 30 per frame from -0x20000; the first past `0x7FFFFF` wins. Horse names (40) come from the table at `0x800BB534` and text at `0x800556A8` (letters 0 = A, 0x20 = a). The plugin shows a Race box on Spring 18 (season 0, day index 17) or while the race code is loaded: name, odds and win chance from 1000 simulated races per race (fixed seed, about 30 ms once per race); the likely winner is green. In the user's save, race 1 matched the bet screen (Fuji 19, BeeBop 25, Adios 2, Tangle 8, Lava 18, MayDay 1); the sim gave Adios 99.9%. The sim has not been checked against a real race result yet, and other race days (if any) are not known. Deployed as `ps1-player-00117-d2q`.

## Basket

Basket (found 10 November 2026 from the user's saves, read only): the user added 2 crops between each of three saves and `0x800712C0` went 14, 16, 18. Layout: u16 count at `0x800712C0`, 25 u16 counts by kind from `0x800712C2`, u32 sale value at `0x800712F4` (580 for 3 turnips and 5 potatoes, 1080 for 18 turnips). The add code at `0x800F3E44` refuses at 30 and adds the item's price (item records at the pointer in `0x8006C858`, 40 bytes each, price u16 at +0x12). Kinds come from `0x800F3C9C`: items 0x41-0x4F are kinds 0-14 (Turnip to Green pepper, seed order; prices 60, 80, 60, 30, 250, ... match guides); kinds 15-21 group sizes and gold versions and were named from their prices (Egg, Mayonnaise, Wool, Milk, Cheese, Yarn, Fish); kinds 22-24 are items 0x5D-0x5F (100G each): `0x800F3C9C` sends items 93 and up through a jump table at `0x800BA004`, and for these three the code at `0x800F3D2C` returns item - 71. Almar's Guides CodeBreaker item list (https://almarsguides.com/retro/walkthroughs/PS1/Games/HarvestMoonBackToNature/CodeBreaker/ItemModifierDigits/, read 10 November 2026; it also gives 041 Turnip, 07C Copper, 138 Junk Ore, matching the game) names them 05D Blue Grass, 05E Red Grass, 05F Green Grass, so the plugin shows Blue grass, Red grass and Green grass (named in `ps1-player-00122-jmp`). Each item record (40 bytes) has a number at +0xE (Turnip 0, Potato 1, items 93-95 38, 40, 39) that does not index the text list at `0x80054478` (that list starts Turnip seeds, Potato seeds). The user held item 28 (named Brush by the public list) while carrying the basket in every basket save; that name was not changed. The plugin's Basket line shows `count/30` (red when full), each kind's count and `Worth`; Mine ores add to the count and the value but to no kind: on 10 November 2026 the user threw ores in at place 67 and the saves read count 1 then 2, worth 15G then 30G, with every kind at 0. So the basket does not record which ore it was. The add function at `0x800F3E44` (read from the user's save of 10 November 2026) writes only three things: it adds the item's price (`0x800D603C`, which only reads) to the u32 value, adds 1 to the count, and adds 1 to the kind count only when `0x800F3C9C` gives a kind (it gives -1 for ores). No item id is stored. Prices from the item table at the pointer in `0x8006C858` (Turnip 60 and Potato 80 match): Junk Ore 1, Coin 10, Copper 15, Silver 20, Gold 25, Mystrile 40, Orichalcum 50, Adamantite 50, Power Berry and item 99 0. The user's 2 ores at 15G each fit 2 Copper. The plugin shows the gap between the count and the kinds as Other, and hides the line when empty or when the kinds add up to more than the count. Deployed as `ps1-player-00118-9bt`; the Other line in `ps1-player-00121-vs2`.

Chicken feeding (found 11 November 2026 from the game's code and the user's three iCloud saves, read only): coop feed boxes are 10 bytes at `0x8007130E` (non-zero = filled; `0x800D0EA4` fills one). Barn fodder boxes are bytes at `0x800712F8`. Chicken records are `0xB4` bytes from `0x800742EC`: +0 kind (`0x109` hen, `0x10A` chick), +0x16 love (s16), +0x30 place (4 = in the coop, 1 = outside on the farm), +0x34 flags (bit 0 fed today, `0x10` lays an egg next morning, `0x100` sick), +0xAC outside ticks, +0xB0 age in days. The new-day step (`0x800DDDFC` → `0x800CECB0`) runs `0x800E21E4` for each chicken. A hen in the coop that is not yet fed takes the first filled box, from box 0 up, and empties it. Boxes are not tied to a chicken. A hen outside skips the box check. Fed: love +10 (max 255) and an egg next morning (love under 100 normal, under 200 good, 200+ high, golden with flag `0x40`). Not fed: love −20 (min 0) and no egg; it also sets a game-wide "hungry" flag. Then the fed flag and the outside ticks reset. A separate repeating step (`0x800DE334` → `0x800CEBC8` → `0x800E20F0`) handles hens outside (place 1): in rain (weather `0x8007AD00` = 1) love −5 each run; otherwise outside ticks +1, and at 5 the hen counts as fed. Nothing happens outside in winter (season 3). How often that step runs was not confirmed. In the saves, box 1 was full on Spring 21 at 10 PM and the hen's love went 50 → 70 by Spring 23 (two fed nights).

The plugin's Hens line (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-feed-open`) shows the hen count and turns red when fewer coop boxes are full than unfed hens in the coop. Tapping it lists each hen: In coop, Outside n/5, or Away, and Fed, Needs feed, Not fed, 🌧️ Rain or ❄️ No grass. Chicks are left out. On 11 November 2026, `make check` passed, and the user's Spring 23, 1:50 AM save, loaded in a separate muted Chrome tab at localhost:8799, showed the feed line and Amy · In coop · Needs feed. A real night was not played through, and the outside rows were checked only with fake RAM. Not deployed.

Chicken love changes found in code (11 November 2026, read only): fed at the new day +10, not fed −20, outside in rain −5 per outside run. Two chicken actions in the action table at `0x800B96C8` each take 10 love: `0x800E2C64` (startles the hen and stores the player's position) and `0x800E2D3C` (knocks her away). They look like tool hits; which tools was not traced. `0x8011FDCC` adds a set amount to every chicken and barn animal (max 255, via `0x8011FF74`); its trigger was not traced. No love gain was found for picking a hen up, but the pick-up code itself was not found, so this is not proven.
