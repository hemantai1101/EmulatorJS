# Harvest Moon notes

Research notes for the Harvest Moon: Back to Nature plugin (`hosting/ps1/plugins/hm-stamina-bar.js`). RAM addresses, what the game's code does, and what has or has not been checked.

## Mobile panel

on touch screens or pages up to 900px wide, only stamina, fatigue and **More** show at first. **More** is a small text link that opens the full panel; **Close** shrinks it. Basket starts with just its count and bar; tap its row to show or hide items and worth. The full view scrolls and keeps Close in reach. Tap **Hearts** to open the girls' hearts. Desktop keeps the full view. On 10 November 2026, `make check` and separate muted desktop Chrome tests passed at 852×393, 568×320 and 390×844 with touch emulation, plus 1280×800 with a mouse. Small view cleared L1/L2/D-pad and L2 received touch input; More/Close and Hearts worked. Preview screenshots used the supplied game photo as a still and fake RAM. No real iPhone check. Deployed as `ps1-player-00123-474` on 10 November 2026; live index and plugin files matched the build with both isolation headers kept.

## Stamina and fatigue

Plugin `plugins/hm-stamina-bar.js` (Harvest Moon stamina bar) is written here. It shows stamina and fatigue bars for Harvest Moon: Back to Nature (US, SLUS-01115) and only reads RAM. Addresses come from public cheat lists: stamina `0x80071A12`, max stamina `0x80071A14`, fatigue `0x80071A16` (u16). On 9 November 2026 a console read of the user's live game gave stamina 100, max 100, fatigue 18. The game's code caps fatigue at 100 (clamp at `0x800D62EC`; at 6 AM a value of 100 or more becomes 99) and changes behaviour at 50, 70 and 80 (`0x800CD4E4`). No code raises that cap, so the Mystic Berry does not change the bar's top. Fatigue shows amber from 50 and red from 80. Other cheat-list addresses (not built into a plugin): money u32 `0x80071A5C`, month `0x80070CF2`, day `0x80070CF4`, hour `0x80070CF8`. `make check` runs `hm-stamina-bar.test.cjs` with fake RAM. The box was checked with fake RAM in the built-in browser. On 9 November 2026 it was also pasted into a separate muted Chrome tab of the live site that started the user's last game (Harvest Moon, Spring 3, 6 AM): it showed Stamina 100/100 and Fatigue 18. The game was not played, so a stamina drop was not seen yet. The tab was closed within about a minute. The day value is 0-based (RAM 2 = day 3).

## Girls' hearts

Tapping **Hearts** opens the girls' hearts (tapping the stats does not); localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-hearts-open` keeps that choice. Affection is u16 at `0x80076224 + villager * 0x1D4`; the girls' villager numbers (Karen 3, Ann 7, Elli 9, Mary 14, Popuri 20) come from the table at `0x80055988`. Heart steps come from the game's code at `0x80022F1C`: below 5000, 20000, 30000, 40000, 50000, 60000, then 60000 and up (7 levels). The colour names (black, purple, blue, green, yellow, orange, red) come from fan guides, not the code. Mary's 0 was read from the address the game's own code uses. On 9 November 2026 a chat with Mary in a muted test tab (saving blocked) set `0x80071A22` (the villager being talked to) to 14, confirming Mary is villager 14. That chat changed no affection value, so a plain chat may not add points, at least not a second one on the same day. The game's add-affection function at `0x8011FF0C` (overlay code seen in the library scene) adds to the same `+0x19EC` field for any villager and caps it at 65535. All seven saved Harvest Moon states up to 9 November 2026 21:20 had Mary (villager 14) at 0; her `+0x1A` flag word has bit `0x400` set, which an event sets together with affection at `0x800EA150`. The user reported a Mary event worth +2000 points; it was not found in those saves.

## Held tool and water

Held count (found 11 November 2026 from the game's code, read only): the item menu draw at `0x800FB510` shows a slot's u16 at +4 as a count when `0x800D583C` returns 2. That is byte +2 of the item's 40-byte record behind the pointer at `0x8006C858` (the same table the Basket uses). In an old RAM dump of the user's game, type 2 was items 34-54 (the seeds), 80, 296-298, 376 and 377. Stacking at `0x800FB418` caps the count at 99. The Holding line adds ` ×n` for type-2 items with a count from 1 to 99. Not yet seen with seeds in the user's game. Chicken records hold `0x10A` for a chick at +0 (0x16 bytes before love); the Animals list shows chicks as 🐣 and hens as 🐔.

Water: items are 6-byte slots (u16 id, u16 value at +4). The held item is at `0x80071A84`; the 24 tool slots start at `0x80070EC2`. Tool item numbers 1-57 (1-5 sickles, 6-10 hoes, 11-15 axes, 16-20 hammers, 21-25 cans, then animal tools, seeds, Ocarina, Fishing Rod, Fishing Pole) come from the Harvest Moon Paradise GameShark page (https://harvestmoonparadise.com/hmpb/page.php?id=32); the basic tools match the user's saves. That page also lists `80071A88 0064` as Watering Can Always Full. The plugin's Holding line shows this name. The basic watering can is item 21 and its value is the water left: 0 at the start, 100 after filling, minus 1 per watering (from the user's saves on 9 November 2026). On 10 November 2026 the user's upgraded can was held as item 25 (Mystrile Can) with value 100 when full and value field +2 = 4. The Water line now treats items 21-25 the same, as water left out of 100; how fast upgraded cans drop per use has not been seen. Deployed as `ps1-player-00093-xqn`; see DEPLOYMENT.md.

## Mine floors

Mine (read from the user's save on 9 November 2026, in a muted test tab with saving blocked; not in the plugin): each floor is a 10 x 10 grid. Header at `0x8012D110` (u16): +0 current floor, 0-based (went 0 to 1 when the user took the stairs; a reset clears all 10 floors' grids), +2 stairs chance in percent (10 on a new floor, +2 after each miss), +4/+6 a row/col used to keep stairs away on kinds other than 0, +8/+A the found stairs' row/col, +C one byte per kind set once stairs are found. Tile state bytes start at `0x8012D126 + floor * 100`, index row * 10 + col: 0 not dug, 1 dug hole, 2 stairs. The hoe handler at `0x8011337C` (overlay code) decides what a tile holds only when it is dug: edge tiles give a plain hole, other tiles roll `rand(100) < chance` for stairs while none are found. So nothing hidden is stored before digging. This block stays in memory outside the mine. The place number is a u16 behind the pointer at `0x800A0378` (pointer was `0x8009F78C` in every save). The hoe digs mine tiles only at places 55 and 67 (check at `0x800C64DC`); the user's mine saves read 67 and two farm-time saves read 19. The plugin's Mine line shows `Floor <+0 plus 1>` only at 55 or 67. Do not use `0x8006C3FA`: it changed between 25 and 100 while the player stood still. Deployed as `ps1-player-00098-rs6`.

## Weather

Weather is u16: today at `0x8007AD00`, tomorrow at `0x8007AD02`. In the user's ten saves of 9 November 2026 (Spring 6 to 10), each day's tomorrow value became the next day's today value, and tomorrow's value stayed the same from 6 AM to night. Values 0 Sunny and 1 Rain were seen; 2 Snow, 3 Typhoon and 4 Blizzard come from a public cheat list (vcgamers.com) and are not checked. The plugin's Tmrw line shows an emoji (☀️ 🌧️ ❄️ 🌀 🌨️) with the name as a tooltip, and hides for other values. How the game picks tomorrow's weather has not been traced.

## Mine items

Mine items (read from the user's save of 9 November 2026 at Spring 14; the code and tables were in memory on the farm): when a dug tile is not stairs, `0x80113A20` (overlay code) rolls `rand(100)` (`0x80012A34`) and walks 8 slots, adding each slot's percent until the roll is below the sum. Percents are s16, 8 per floor, at `0x80127FE8 + floor * 16` for place 67 and `0x80128088 + floor * 16` for place 55. Item numbers are at `0x80128128` (67: 0, 102, 312, 124, 123, 122, 96, 103) and `0x80128138` (55: 0, 312, 102, 96, 97, 98, 103, 99). Item 0 is nothing. At 67, item 103 is given once (flag 97); at 55, 103 once (flag 98) and 99 once (flag 96); a repeat gives nothing. Place 67 floor 1 is 40/30/20/10 for slots 0-3; floor 10 is 0/0/10/30/25/20/10/5. Place 55 floor 9 is 0/0/0/0/25/25/0/50, and its floor 10 row starts with 100 (always nothing). Item names come from Almar's Guides CodeBreaker item list (hex ids, read from a 21 March 2024 web.archive.org copy; the live page returns 404): 96 Mystrile, 97 Orichalcum, 98 Adamantite, 102 Coin, 103 Power Berry, 122 Gold, 123 Silver, 124 Copper, 312 Junk Ore. Item 99 is not on that list; the plugin calls it Rare item. The game's own name list in memory on the farm (u16 text, letter = value + 65, `0xFFFF` ends a name) held food names but no ores. The plugin shows each floor's odds under the Mine line from copies of these tables; starred items are given once. Edge tiles and tiles within 2 of found stairs give a plain hole with no item roll.

Game flags (found 11 November 2026, read only): `0x8001AF44` reads flag n as bit `n & 31` of the u32 at `0x8006C870 + (n >> 5) * 4`; `0x8001AF70` sets it. Mine flags 96, 97 and 98 are bits 0, 1 and 2 of `0x8006C87C`. In a browser `.state` (RASTATE, one `MEM ` chunk), RAM address 0 is at file byte 36917 (`0x9035`). All 10 of the user's browser saves (Spring 30, 11 PM, read on the live site) had flag 97 set (Spring mine Power Berry already found) and flag 98 clear (Lake mine berry not found); max stamina was 130. Eating a Power Berry runs `0x800D63C4` (item 103's use function, at +0x1C of its 40-byte record; args 10 and -1 at +0x20): it adds 10 to max stamina (cap 255), fills stamina, and adds 1 to the u16 berry count at `0x800712BC`. No other item uses this function. All 10 saves had the count at 3. Berry sources other than the mines are bits of the u16 at `0x800712BE`. Found 11 November 2026 from the disc (`A_FILE.BIN` inside the user's game file in browser storage, read only) and the user's save: event scripts are 16-byte commands (u8 op at +0, s16 args at +4 and +6, s32 at +8 and +12; handler table `0x800B975C`). Op 81 with first arg 6 tests (third arg non-zero) or sets (0) the bit mask in the second arg (`0x800E9D28`). Op 31 with 103 gives the berry; op 2 shows message n, read as an 8 KB slot at `A_FILE.BIN` offset 81313792 + n * 8192 (`0x800229F4`). Bits: 4 horse-race prize bought for medals (code at `0x80116920`), 5 festival winner prize (message 1237), 7 giving the girl all the flowers she wants (messages 382-383), 8 Harvest Goddess offerings (messages 523-540), 9 not cutting the old cedar on Mother's Hill (messages 846-849). The TV shopping delivery (messages 889 and 881) sets no bit here. The user's saves had `0x800712BE` = `0x300` (bits 8 and 9), so the three berries were the Spring mine, the Harvest Goddess and the cedar. Flags 99 and 100 are set by `0x80112DA8` when places 55 and 72 are entered, not by berries.

## Shops

Shops: the weekday is u16 at `0x80070CF6` (0 Sunday; it went up by one each day in the user's saves, and the day-name code at `0x8001AAB8` lists "Sun" first; its caller at `0x800FB73C` reads this address), season at `0x80070CF2`, hour at `0x80070CF8`. The plugin's "Shops" toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-shops-open`) lists shop hours with a green dot when open now. Hours come from fan guides (harvestmoonbacktonatureguide.com shops page; Supermarket 9 AM to 6 PM from the fan wiki), not the game's code, and guides disagree on the Supermarket and Saibara. Storms and Saibara's earlier hours with friendship are not handled. Whole-day closures (12 November 2026): a shop row shows a red "Closed" on its rest weekday, and Kai outside summer. The day of season is u16 `0x80070CF4` (0 is day 1). On daytime festivals the toggle adds "🎉" and the festival name, and every shop shows "🎉 Closed". Festival dates come from the fan wiki (wiki.ranchstory.farm), not the game's code: Spring 1, 8, 18, 22; Summer 1, 7, 12, 20; Fall 9, 21; Winter 10. Evening festivals and the Thanksgiving days are left out. Which shops really close on each festival was not checked in game; a quick search of the game code found no festival table. Deployed as `ps1-player-00150-hvx`.

## Animals

Animals: love is a byte; addresses from Almar's Guides CodeBreaker animal codes (archived copy): chickens from `0x80074302` every `0xB4` (10 slots, up to the barn), barn animals from `0x80074A0A` every `0xB8` (20 slots), dog `0x800759FA`, horse `0x80075AEA`. The name is 9 u16 letters 18 bytes before the love byte, padded with `0xFFFF`; empty slots start with `0xFFFF`. In the user's saves up to Spring 15 only the dog ("Bin") was named; its love went 19, 23, 17, 11, 8, 10 over Spring 10 to 15. The plugin's Animals toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-animals-open`) lists named slots whose last name cell is `0xFFFF`, as a bar with `love/255`. Cows and sheep are told apart by their record (see Cows and sheep below).

## Villagers

Villagers (found 10 November 2026 from the user's ten saves, read only): other villagers' friendship is the u16 at `0x80076222 + villager * 0x1D4`, 2 bytes before the girls' affection field. The game's add function at `0x8011FEE0` (just before the affection adder at `0x8011FF0C`; same base `0x80074838`, field `+0x19EA`) caps it at 255. The girls' own `+0x19EA` field was 0 in every save, and the other villagers' `+0x19EC` field was 0. Saved values ran about 46 to 109 and rose over the saves for some villagers (villager 8 from 70 to 109, Barley and May together from 47 to 76 and 77). Names come from the vcgamers.com GameShark list (its "Citizen Affection" codes write this byte); every code address fits `0x80076222 + n * 0x1D4` exactly, and its girl codes (Karen `800767A0` and so on) match the game's girl table. Names were not checked one by one in the game (no name list is in RAM; the talk target `0x80071A22` was `0xFFFF` in every save). The plugin's Villagers toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-villagers-open`) lists 28 of them with a bar and `value/255`: 1 Jeff, 2 Sasha, 4 Saibara, 5 Gray, 6 Doug, 8 Doctor, 10 Carter, 11 Cliff, 12 Basil, 13 Anna, 15 Barley, 16 May, 17 Thomas, 18 Lillia, 19 Rick, 21 Duke, 22 Manna, 23 Ellen, 24 Gotz, 25 Kai, 26 Zack, 28 Harris, 29 Kano (the list says "Canoe"), 30 Gourmet (list: "Gourmand"), 31 Louis, 32 Greg, 34 Stu, 35 Won. Villager 8 is the Doctor: on 10 November 2026 the user gave him a gift, and the auto-save during that talk had the talk target `0x80071A22` = 8 with friendship 160; the next save (4 seconds later) had 169. No other villager's friendship changed then. The cheat list's Doctor address `0x800990C2` does not fit the layout and is not used. The Doctor was added to the list in `ps1-player-00120-6dq`. Not listed: villagers 0, 26, 27 and 33 have no name on the list; 36 ("Your Baby") is left out; the Harvest Sprites' addresses do not fit the layout. Deployed as `ps1-player-00114-gc4`.

## Harvest Sprites

Harvest Sprites (found 10 November 2026 from the user's saves, read only): 7 records of `0xF8` bytes from `0x8007A638` (code at `0x8010C6A0` clears 1736 bytes there; loops at `0x8010C95C` walk 7 records). Each record holds the sprite's name at +4 (u16 letters, `0xFFFF` ends) and friendship at +0x16; the daily code at `0x8010CB6C` adds or takes points and `0x8010CC78` keeps it within 0-255. Names read from the records: Chef, Nappy, Timid, Hoggy, Staid, Bold, Aqua (in that order); these match the vcgamers.com codes ("Diapers" there is Nappy). All seven read 50 in the saves up to 10 November 02:59 and 70 in the 03:48 save. The plugin reads each name from RAM and lists the sprites under their own Sprites toggle (localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-sprites-open`), apart from the Villagers list; each bar uses the sprite's outfit colour from fan guides (Chef red, Nappy orange, Hoggy yellow, Timid green, Aqua blue, Staid indigo, Bold purple). Deployed as `ps1-player-00116-k4r`.

Sprite watering (found 14 November 2026 from the code in the user's 13 November 2026 18:26 iCloud save, read only; not watched in a game). The save's RAM starts at file offset `0x9035`. The tile picker `0x80110658` rolls `rand(n)` (`0x80012A34`, n = 1125 for the field or 49 for the Hothouse), then walks forward tile by tile (left to right, next row, back to tile 0) and takes the first tile that passes. So each pick is random, and a crop right after a long empty stretch is picked more often. The field is searched first; the Hothouse only when no field tile passes. Daily watering (`0x8010D46C`) uses check `0x80110838`: a crop that is not grass (item `0x36`) on soil `0x168` or `0x16A`; the hire check `0x80110890` also takes `0x169` and `0x16B`. Which soil values are wet was not checked. Each run waters `rand(5) + skill / 10` tiles, where the water skill is u16 +0xB0 of the sprite record, +1 per day on a water job (`0x8010CC14`, max 255). The run is event 4 in the time table at `0x80122E80` (minute 0 of every hour), run by `0x8010C95C` for each sprite and `0x8010CAD4`, which only works when the hour (`0x80070CF8`) is 6-20, the flag at `0x80128B50` is 1 (meaning not known), and job bit `0x40` is clear. The new day clears `0x40` (`0x8010CD58`), so no work on the hire day. That is 15 runs a day, about 30 tiles at skill 0. All sprites had skill 0 in that save. The field is 45 by 25 tiles and every tile is ground (`0x167` plain, `0x168`/`0x16A` tilled). The plugin shows the water skill as `💧n` after each sprite's name in the open Sprites list; the tooltip gives the tiles per hour and about how many a day. On 14 November 2026, `make check` passed and a separate local page with the user's save RAM (two skills set by hand to 7 and 23) in the built-in browser showed the numbers. Deployed as `ps1-player-00167-8f9`. Harvest and animal skills (found 14 November 2026, same save): u16 +0xAE is the harvest skill and +0xB2 the animal skill, each +1 per day on that job (max 255, `0x8010CBD8`, `0x8010CC50`). Each hourly harvest run picks `rand(3) + harvest / 50` ripe crops (`0x8010D350`), about 15 a day at skill 0. Animal care runs steps for animal kinds 5, 4 and 1 (`0x8010D648`, `0x8010D748`, `0x8010D850`); each step's count adds `animal / 50` or `animal / 100`. What each step does to the animals was not traced. In the save, harvest skills were Chef 16, Nappy 8, Hoggy 16, others 0; animal skills were all 0. The plugin's open Sprites list is a table (`ps1-player-00172-6xn`): a header row with 🌾 💧 🐄, then one line per sprite with name and job, the friendship bar (number in its tooltip), and the three skills as numbers with a thin bar out of 255 under each (harvest lime, water blue, animals pink), each with a tooltip. The skill for the sprite's current job is bright, a zero skill is dim, and a value over 255 leaves an empty cell. The Villagers and Sprites lists each use one grid for all rows (rows are `display: contents`), so the name column fits the longest name and the bar fills the rest (`ps1-player-00173-k9h`). Each sprite's and villager's friendship bar is 12px tall with the number drawn on its right end (white with a dark outline; class `obar`); the full `n/255` is in the bar's tooltip. Animal love bars (tooltip `Love n/255`, bar 102px) and heart bars (tooltip `Affection n/65,535`) use the same `obar` style with the number on the bar; checked on a separate local page with the save's RAM in the built-in browser on 14 November 2026. The Water row sits with Stamina and Fatigue above the More button, so it also shows in the collapsed phone view; checked at 844×390 on that local page with a can set by hand. There is no More/Close button: on phones, a tap on the panel opens or closes the details; taps on list toggles, rows and links stop there and do not close the panel. Skill cells use classes `sk-harvest`, `sk-water`, `sk-animals`; a plain `animals` class would pick up the Animals list style. On 14 November 2026, `make check` passed and a separate local page with the save's RAM in the built-in browser showed all seven rows on one line, with a job icon too. Deployed as `ps1-player-00168-dkw`.

Hiring sprites (found 12 November 2026 from the disc's code, read only; not tested in a game): sprite text is message bank part 4 of `A_FILE.BIN` (8 KB slots, text at +0x1800). Chef's job menu is message 327; the shared "Nothing to ask for help with..." is 329. The job code is in part 11, which runs at `0x800B7E20`; it loads 329 at `0x8010E9B4`. Each job scans the field (`0x80071DFC`, 1125 tiles of 8 bytes, 45 wide) and then the Hothouse (`0x80074124`, 49 tiles, only when `0x80070D0C` is non-zero) through `0x80110658`. Harvest (`0x80110790`) needs at least one crop at its last growth stage. It compares tile byte +4 with the u16 at +2 of the growth table row named by byte +3 of the item's 40-byte record. Crops that are still growing do not count. Water (`0x80110890`) needs a tile whose soil u16 at +2 is `0x168`-`0x16B`. Animal care checks `0x80110944` with kinds 1, 4 and 5. If no tile passes, the game shows message 329 and no hire starts. The plugin's **Ripe** line runs the same harvest check. It shows `ripe/crops` with 🧚 bright when at least one crop is ripe. The tooltip splits field and Hothouse. The line sits at the bottom of the open Sprites list and hides when that list is closed or there are no crops. Moved there in `ps1-player-00158-nmm`. In the game's tiles, byte +4 is the stage, and the growth row's u16 at +0xC is the crop it gives. Grass counts as a crop, as it does in the game's check. On 12 November 2026, `make check` passed. Two old RAM dumps of the user's game gave 0 of 153 ripe (Spring 17, mostly grass) and 0 of 252 ripe (Summer 6). A ripe crop has not been seen in real RAM yet, and the line has not been seen in a running game. Deployed as `ps1-player-00147-dm5`.

Harvest hire with no ripe crop (13 November 2026; code read from the user's 11 November 2026 Summer browser save, not yet tried in a game): the hire menu step is u16 +0x28 of the sprite's record. For harvest, `0x8010E7E0` sets it to 2 (the days menu), scans the field and then the Hothouse with `0x80110658`, and only if both find nothing writes 4 at `0x8010E818` (`sh v0, 0x28(s0)`, word `0xA6020028`); step 4 shows message 329. The job bits `0x41` are set either way. The scanner returns 0 with the tile when one passes and 1 when none does, and it is shared with the sprites' daily work (other callers at `0x8010D3C8`-`0x8010ED58`), so the ripe test itself is left alone. The plugin's opt-in button **🌾 Hire with no ripe crop** (in the open Sprites list; localStorage `ps1-plugin-hm-any-harvest`) writes a nop over that one word. It writes only when the six words at `0x8010E810` match the hire code (`10400002 24020004 A6020028 960200B8 00000000 34420041`, found once in RAM), checks every 250 ms because part 11 is reloaded, and puts the word back when turned off or when the plugin stops. A sprite hired this way has a harvest job; on a day with no ripe crop the daily work's scan finds nothing, as after a normal first harvest day. On 13 November 2026, `make check` passed. Not yet seen in a running game. Deployed as `ps1-player-00159-5jq`.

Sprite jobs (found 12 November 2026 from the disc's code, read only): the job lives in the sprite's own record (`0x8007A638 + k * 0xF8`; +0xAC is the sprite index). Picking a job sets bit 0x40 plus 1 (harvest), 2 (water) or 4 (animals) in u16 +0xB8 (`0x8010E81C`, `0x8010E87C`, `0x8010E8DC`). Picking days writes u16 +0xB4 = 2, 4 or 8 for 1 day, 3 days or 1 week (`0x8010E988`). "Oh, never mind" clears bits `0x47` (`0x8010E9F8`). Each new day (`0x8010CCB0`) takes 1 off +0xB4. The sprite works while it stays above 0; at 0 the job bits are cleared. So +0xB4 - 1 work days are left. The plugin adds the job icon (🌾 harvest, 💧 water, 🐄 animals) after the sprite's name, and to the closed Sprites toggle. Hovering shows `Harvest · 3 more days` or `last day today`. Next to the icon in the open list, a small label shows the work days left (`3d`), or `today` on the last day. The label was deployed as `ps1-player-00155-nn7`. In the two old RAM dumps and the 11 November 2026 Summer 4 browser save, every sprite had +0xB4 and +0xB8 at 0 (no one hired). On 12 November 2026, `make check` passed. A real hire has not been seen in RAM yet. Deployed as `ps1-player-00147-dm5`.

## Horse race

Horse race (found 10 November 2026 from the disc's code and the user's Spring 18 save, read only): 3 races of 6 horses, 0xE bytes each from `0x8007AD4E` (race * 0x54): u16 id, speed, whip, style, push, odds. The current race is u16 at `0x8007AD4C` (0-2; 3 means the player rides, using row 2 with id `0xFFFF`). Code at `0x8002206C` rolls them at a new game and on race day (the user's Spring 17 and Spring 18 saves held different horses): speed from per-lane ranges at `0x80055944`, whip 0-255 at random, style and push fixed per horse id (`0x800558A4`), odds = 1 + (best speed - speed)^2 / 1000 + rand(4). The race is mini-game 7 (archive part 17 of `A_FILE.BIN`, loaded at `0x800B7E20`, which then holds 7 and `0x800B7EB8`); betting runs in town code, so that sign is not there at the bet screen. Every frame each horse starts at base pace 128 + 512 * speed / 25500; every 6th frame stamina +5, each horse decides to whip (table at `0x800BB710`, style picks early or late pushing, push applies when another horse is within `0x5A000` ahead), and a whip costs 40 stamina and adds pace * (whip + 150) / 255 for that frame. Position adds pace * 4096 / 30 per frame from -0x20000; the first past `0x7FFFFF` wins. Horse names (40) come from the table at `0x800BB534` and text at `0x800556A8` (letters 0 = A, 0x20 = a). The plugin shows a Race box on Spring 18 (season 0, day index 17) or while the race code is loaded: name, odds and win chance from 1000 simulated races per race (fixed seed, about 30 ms once per race); the likely winner is green. In the user's save, race 1 matched the bet screen (Fuji 19, BeeBop 25, Adios 2, Tangle 8, Lava 18, MayDay 1); the sim gave Adios 99.9%. The sim has not been checked against a real race result yet, and other race days (if any) are not known. Deployed as `ps1-player-00117-d2q`.

Fishing (found 11 November 2026 from the disc's code, read only; not played). Fish are items 163 small (50G), 162 medium (120G) and 161 large (200G); prices from the item table, and the basket counts all three as Fish. The fishing code is in part 11 (`0x8011FFA8`, state in player +0x3C, data at `0x8012DB48`: +0 bite window, +4 countdown, +8 spot). Casting first throws a line item (390) through the throw code (`0x800CC4E0`); only water whose tile kind (`0x800BF3F4`) is 1 counts, so the farm pond (kind 2) and kind-3 water give nothing. A countdown (`0x801201dc`/`0x80120358`) is picked by the minute of the day (u16 `0x80070D02`, 930 at 3:30 PM in a save) from the table at `0x801285F4`, for spots 0-4: 22:00-7:59 15/60/60/150/30, 8:00-17:59 60/180/180/210/90, 18:00-21:59 30/120/120/210/60 ticks. On a bite the player has `rand(25) + 5` ticks (spot 3: `rand(10) + 5`) to react, or the fish gets away. Reacting early pulls the line in with nothing. The steps (player +0x3C): 0 waiting, 1 bite, 2 pulling in with nothing, 3 pulling in after a bite.
